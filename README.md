# ScamBait X-Ray

Scammers don't hack you. They read your bio.

Paste your own public bio and see what a scammer would pick from it: college, city, phone, email, birthday. The tool shows a lead score, writes a synthetic scam message built from your details, and lets you black out each detail to watch the risk drop. It also has a Scam or Real? mini-game.

**Demo:** <https://j0sankum4r.github.io/ScamBait-X-ray/>

## Privacy
Runs entirely in your browser. No backend, no login, nothing is sent or saved.

## Limits
It only reads the text you paste. The score is a rough estimate, not a guarantee of safety. All examples are fictional.
